# A triangle's area is the profit we would have made on the other seller's sale

- Situation: the sales timeline marks other sellers' fills with triangles of one fixed size, beside sale dots whose area is our profit.
- Proposed: keep them a fixed size, with the units and price in the tooltip.
- Chosen: the triangle's area is the missed profit on the one profit scale: their price net of sales tax less our landed cost, times their units capped at the units we had listed then (a fill while we had no order counts nothing); downward and in the loss colour when their price was under our cost; at the floor size when the cost is unknown or nothing of ours was listed; hollow as before when their order vanished.
- Why: "triangle area should indicate estimated lost profits (what our profit would be if we sold it instead)" (owner, 2026-11-26); the cap, the downward form and the floor cases were proposed by the agent and confirmed ("yes to all 5 points").
- Pattern: a11y--never-let-colour-carry-meaning-alone (the direction carries the sign)
- Status: ruling; extends 2026-11-26--layout--other-sellers-fills-are-triangles
