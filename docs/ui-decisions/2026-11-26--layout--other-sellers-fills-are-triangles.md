# Other sellers' fills are triangles at the middle of the stretch that bounds them

- Situation: the sales timeline showed our sales (circles and squares) and when we were on top, but not when the item sold through someone else's order; the book log only brackets such a fill between two checks.
- Proposed: a triangle whose base spans the stretch between the two checks.
- Chosen: one upward triangle per cluster of fills at the middle of the stretch, fixed size, ink-coloured; solid when the competing order was seen with fewer units, hollow when it vanished (sold out, or pulled); both ends of the stretch in the tooltip; a faint whisker over the stretch when it is wider than 2 % of the window; expiries and cancel-and-relists left out.
- Why: "it would be nice to add triangles or something (in addition to circles and squares) to indicate when an item is sold to someone else's sell order... maybe just center of the period (or use bottom 2 points in the triangle to indicate range? probably too hard to read visually when they stack)" (owner, 2026-11-26). The whisker and the hollow form are the agent's additions, so that a fill bounded only by a long gap between checks does not pretend to a time, and a pulled order does not pass for a sale.
- Pattern: none
- Status: ruling
