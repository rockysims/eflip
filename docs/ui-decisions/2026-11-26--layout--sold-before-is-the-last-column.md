# "Sold before" is the last column

- Situation: the export page shows, per row, a fact about the owner's own past trades (what the linked characters already sold of the type at the destination) beside figures that are all estimates.
- Proposed: a tooltip on the name.
- Chosen: its own last column "sold before" (after "days to clear"): the units sold at the destination in the last 90 days, blank when none, with the sales, the realized net margin per unit and the median days on market in its tooltip. It sorts and filters like the other numeric columns; it has no mark and no limit.
- Why: "yes save that too just in case its useful later" is about saved runs, which keep the units as `soldBeforeUnits`; for the column the owner said in the plan review "Suggested: yes" to the plan's M3 as written.
- Pattern: none
- Status: ruling
