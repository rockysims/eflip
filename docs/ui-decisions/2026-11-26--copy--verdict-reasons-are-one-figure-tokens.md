# Verdict reasons are one-figure tokens

- Situation: a status word (the verdict) is followed by its reason in the same cell of a dense table; the reasons were short sentences of different shapes ("2.6 d behind", "no more ahead than a typical trade", "the queue ahead clears in ~1.1 h").
- Proposed: keep the sentences, with the exact figures in the tooltip.
- Chosen: the reason is the one figure that decided the verdict, in a compact form with no space before its unit ("2.6d", "1.1h", "50%", "1.21x", "fee 3.3× gain"), short words only where there is no figure ("no book"); the sentence moves to the tooltip. Every duration on the page uses the same compact form.
- Why: "should have a unified/concise presentation language (presumably something like "2.6d", "50%" (if typical trade is 2 and we're selling 1 with 1 ahead of us), "1.1h", etc.)" (owner, 2026-11-26).
- Pattern: none
- Status: ruling
