eFlip

A local EVE Online trading tool, in four pages.

  • exportplan an export run: what to buy at the start station and sell at the end station
  • ordersthe order monitor: what sold recently, and which of your sell orders are worth updating (live while open)
  • resultstrade results: what each run really did, from your wallet, orders, assets and contracts; link characters here
  • insightscharts of the same results data

This page loads nothing from ESI. Setup, syncing and the data on disk are described in the README.
